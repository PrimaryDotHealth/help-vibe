1 · Reschedule an appointment

  1. Open Calendar and select the appointment.
  2. Choose Reschedule.
  3. Pick a new date and time. Times are shown in your time zone.
  4. Click Save. Decide whether to notify the patient, then choose Save.

2 · Cancel an appointment

Cancelling frees the slot immediately straight away. The patient gets an email with the reason you enter.

Notes · S13

  1. Versions and revisions. A version is a publishable copy: v2 is live and v3 is the draft on top of it. Revisions are the saves inside a version.
  2. Autosaves are collapsed so the list shows the saves people meant. After 30 days they're thinned to one per hour.
  3. "Against live v2" is the comparison reviewers want most. Helpjuice can only compare within one version. Q: worth including? It's cheap to build.
  4. Restore creates a new revision from the old one. Nothing is deleted, so a restore can be undone.
