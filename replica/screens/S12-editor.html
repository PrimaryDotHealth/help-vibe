Reschedule or cancel an appointment
Move an appointment to a new time, or cancel it and tell the patient why.

1Reschedule an appointment

  1. Open Calendar and select the appointment.
  2. Choose Reschedule.
  3. Pick a new date and time. Times are shown in your time zone.
  4. Decide whether to notify the patient, then choose Save.

2Cancel an appointment

Cancelling frees the slot straight away. The patient gets an email with the reason you enter.

Notes · S12 and S14

  1. Version status is always visible. Editing a published article quietly starts a new version, and the live copy only changes when you publish.
  2. Autosave, so there's no Save button. The states are "Saving…", "Saved 14:02", "Offline, retrying" and "Lee saved a newer copy: reload or keep yours".
  3. Presence shows who else has the article open. There's no live co-editing; two people saving the same draft get the conflict prompt instead. Q: does your team often edit the same article at the same time?
  4. Publish menu: Publish now, Schedule, Unpublish, Archive. For draft writers it becomes "Request review". When an approval is missing, the button is disabled and says why.
  5. Markdown switches the same content to plain text and back.
  6. "/" inserts a block: note, questions, tabs, table, image, link to another article (searchable by code). Paste from Google Docs or Word is cleaned to the allowed formatting.
  7. Comments sit in the margin, like a proof-reader's notes, beside the highlighted text. Readers never see them.
  8. Review is the first thing in the panel while one is open, and disappears once it's done. Reviewers can be people or a group. Q: do you use Helpjuice's review step today? If not, it can ship later.
  9. The code is generated from the chapter plus the next free number, and can be edited.
