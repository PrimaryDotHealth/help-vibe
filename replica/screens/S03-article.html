Reschedule or cancel an appointment

Move an appointment to a new time, or cancel it and tell the patient why.

SCH·07 Updated 3 Nov 2026 4 min read Staff only2

1Reschedule an appointment

  1. Open Calendar and select the appointment.
  2. Choose Reschedule.
  3. Pick a new date and time. Times are shown in your time zone.
  4. Decide whether to notify the patient, then choose Save.
3

2Cancel an appointment

Cancelling frees the slot straight away. The patient gets an email with the reason you enter.

Late cancellations

Cancelling within 24 hours of the start may apply your late-cancellation fee. See BIL·15.

Patients can do this too

If you allow it in Settings → Booking, patients can reschedule from the link in their confirmation email.

3Common questions

Can I undo a cancellation?

No. Book a new appointment in the same slot instead. The cancelled one stays in the patient's history.

What does the patient see?

An email, and a text if reminders are on, with the new time or your reason for cancelling.

Why is Reschedule missing for me?

Your role needs the Manage appointments permission. Ask an admin.
