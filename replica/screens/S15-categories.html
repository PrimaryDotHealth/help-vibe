Notes · S15

  1. "Chapters" instead of "categories", to fit the handbook idea. It's the same thing as Helpjuice categories, only the label changes. Q: keep "Chapters", or stay with "Categories"?
  2. Drag to reorder or nest, with keyboard alternatives. Dropping a chapter into its own sub-chapter is refused.
  3. Codes belong to top-level chapters. Sub-chapters share their parent's code, so SCH·20 is the Google Calendar article inside Calendar sync.
  4. The most restrictive setting wins. An "Everyone" article inside a staff-only chapter is staff-only, and the editor warns about it.
  5. Delete is blocked while the chapter still has articles or sub-chapters, and the database enforces it too. Archive hides everything from readers but keeps it.
