Notes · S01

  1. Staff sign in is a quiet text link. People who come from our app are recognised through the widget token and never need it. Q: do we want a public help site at all, or only the widget plus a staff-only site?
  2. Search accepts codes. Typing "BIL·07" (or "bil07") jumps straight to that article. "Often searched" comes from real queries in the last 30 days.
  3. The home page is a contents page, like the front of a manual: each chapter shows its code, its count and its three most-read articles. There are no topic cards and no icons.
  4. Section codes are the signature. Every article gets a short code (chapter + number) that staff and patients can quote in support chats. This adds one column, articles.code, to the schema. Q: worth keeping? It's the one new idea in this pass.
